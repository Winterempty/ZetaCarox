# Business health / ดูแนวโน้มธุรกิจ

Use this page when you are unsure what to work on first. It summarizes existing records; it does not change them, send reminders, order products or use an external AI service.

## How to use it

1. Open **Business health** from the sidebar, or use **View business health** on Overview.
2. Read **This month so far** to see entered activity up to today's business date.
3. Review the comparison of the last two **completed** months. The incomplete current month is excluded from that comparison.
4. Choose sales, customer payments, expenses or recorded cash difference in **Show on graph**. Open **Show exact monthly amounts** for the numbers behind it.
5. Choose one item under **What should I do next?** Each includes the evidence, a suggested check and a button to open the relevant records.

English is the default for a new browser. Choose ไทย in Language for plain Thai guidance. Text-size controls work on this page too.

## What the numbers mean

- **Sales before tax:** subtotal of non-void invoices, grouped by issue date. It is invoiced sales, not money collected or a certified revenue-recognition calculation.
- **Customer payments:** recorded payment amounts grouped by receipt date, including invoice tax where collected.
- **Recorded expenses:** non-void expense amounts grouped by paid date. Purchase receipt alone does not enter a cash expense.
- **Recorded cash difference:** customer payments minus recorded expenses. It excludes opening bank balances, financing and other unentered movements. It is not profit, annual tax or bank balance.
- **Month comparisons:** latest completed month versus the immediately preceding completed month. A percentage is shown only when both periods have records for that metric and the prior amount is positive. Missing data is labeled rather than treated as a confirmed decline. Zero and negative bases show an absolute change without a misleading percentage.
- **Graph gaps:** no source records for that metric in that month. A gap is not proof that the business had zero activity. Recorded zero cash differences still appear as points.
- **Colors:** higher recorded sales, receipts or cash difference is green and lower is red. Expense changes stay neutral. These are metric directions, not an overall business rating; higher sales do not by themselves prove higher profit.

Missing, backdated, edited or voided records can change the graph. The system cannot verify that you have entered every transaction. There are no forecasts or year-to-year comparisons in this release.

## Checks and actions

- Overdue invoices: sum the remaining unpaid balance and open the oldest overdue active invoice. Confirm whether payment arrived before following up.
- Low stock: active products at or below their saved reorder point; check incoming orders before buying more.
- Pricing: active products with selling price below positive reference cost. Confirm current costs before making price changes.
- Cash difference: last completed month's entered expenses exceed receipts. Check completeness and review categories before making decisions.
- Sales decline: recorded sales fell across two months that both have sales records. Review missing invoices, volume and price changes.
- Soon-due invoices: unpaid active invoices due today through seven days from today.
- Zero reference costs: check whether zero is correct or data is missing.
- Incomplete sales history: keep recording activity before relying on growth comparisons.

The action list is a fixed, transparent set of rules. It does not assess solvency, recommend loans or investments, calculate tax, or certify that a business is healthy.

## วิธีใช้งานแบบง่าย

เปิดเมนู **ดูแนวโน้มธุรกิจ** แล้วเริ่มจากรายการในหัวข้อ **ควรทำอะไรต่อ?** เช่น ติดตามใบแจ้งหนี้ที่เลยกำหนด ตรวจสอบสินค้าใกล้หมด หรือดูรายจ่ายหมวดที่สูงที่สุด แต่ละรายการมีเหตุผลและปุ่มเปิดข้อมูลที่เกี่ยวข้อง

กราฟใช้ข้อมูลเดือนที่สิ้นสุดแล้ว เพื่อไม่ให้เดือนที่ยังไม่ครบดูเหมือนยอดตก เลือกได้ว่าจะดูยอดขาย เงินรับ รายจ่าย หรือส่วนต่างเงินเข้าและเงินออก หากยังไม่มีข้อมูล ระบบจะบอกตรง ๆ ไม่สร้างแนวโน้มขึ้นมาเอง

สีเขียวหรือสีแดงแสดงทิศทางของตัวเลขนั้น ไม่ได้ตัดสินว่าธุรกิจดีหรือแย่ทั้งหมด และส่วนต่างเงินเข้าและเงินออกไม่ใช่กำไรหรือภาษีประจำปี
